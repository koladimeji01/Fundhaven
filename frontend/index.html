GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Everything you need to feel comfortable getting started.

What can I track with BudgetWise?

You can record expenses, review your spending history, create budgets, track savings goals, and explore financial summaries and reports.

Do I have to connect a bank account?

No. BudgetWise is currently designed around entering your income, expenses, budgets, and goals directly, so you can start without linking a bank account.

Can I use BudgetWise on my phone?

The interface is designed to adapt to phones, tablets, and desktop screens through your web browser.

Can I change how money is displayed?

Settings includes currency and formatting preferences. Some financial displays may use a converted value based on available exchange rates, while stored records remain in their original currency.
